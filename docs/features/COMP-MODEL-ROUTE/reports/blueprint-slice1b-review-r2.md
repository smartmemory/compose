## 2. New specification correctness

**Clean: no new actionable contradiction, identity defect, ordering defect or unimplementable current-tree requirement found.** In particular:

- **Unsupported identities:** Blueprint `:35–37,49,74` distinguishes a supported issuance, connector observation, receipt-based engine observation and coverage gap. The observation does not invent an issuance or treat `legacy:<seq>` as connector identity. Receipt sequence/digest checks, exact Compose crosswalks and unresolved aggregate overlaps prevent the specification from authorizing false certainty or indiscriminate double counting.
- **Binding and launch ordering:** Blueprint `:57–61,76–82` binds the original owner before dispatch, awaits durable connector intent, separates the metadata acknowledgement barrier from fail-open paid delivery, and forbids unresolved-slot redispatch. The startup probe's early position is explicitly handled with the already-bound routing owner (`:71,92`), before the ordinary context/sink setup at `lib/build.js:4259–4290`.
- **Concurrent ownership and revisions:** Blueprint `:39,53,76,86–94,129–131` assigns journal-path locking to participating mutations, original-owner traversal to continuation, and a separate project lock to ledger latest-selection/append. Receipt RPCs occur outside the journal lock and acknowledgement rechecks exact bytes after reload. Append-only resolution heads and ledger versions serve different purposes; no rule requires overwriting an immutable resolution or migrating old receipt ownership to the new physical run.
- **Gate evidence:** Blueprint `:100–110` captures before merge/reset, records the final proposal after merge adjustment, and appends independently witnessed acknowledgement. Nullable future lineage references are completed through later immutable links. Neither current item index, artifact acceptance, a prepared token nor final application success becomes acceptance proof.
- **Cancellation:** Blueprint `:118–123,140–143` requires run authority and per-call evidence, keeps bounded teardown with a single terminal owner, and forbids awaiting teardown from the catch that must release its drain. This fits the ownership ordering at `lib/build.js:6352–6358,6404–6415` and `lib/build-cancel.js:168–205`. Unknown cost/delivery can coexist with settled cancellation without becoming an eligible sample.

These are implementable contracts, not claims that the current S1a tree already provides their behavior.

## 5. Revised test adequacy

**Clean at blueprint level.** Satisfying the complete matrix requires exercising production producers; unit helpers alone cannot meet it.

The adapter matrix requires three distinct populations (`blueprint-slice1b.md:151,176,207`). A seeded routing object beside a bare artifact stub cannot substitute for the successful participating population: that population expressly requires a real validated journal, and the blueprint rejects a separate shadow-journal bypass. Missing-journal tests must establish participation from recorded input/root and assert the named refusal before any model call.

The terminal matrix names concrete Build/GSD entry paths (`:133–143`), requires their invocation of recovery/materialization (`:178`), and is repeated in runner/golden requirements (`:185,206`). The prohibition on seeded joins, acknowledgements or outcomes is global to these tests (`:183,196`). Consequently, a preconstructed final row passed to the materializer does not satisfy a normal/failed/cancelled/paused/exception producer checkbox. Recovery tests may use controlled inference and retained state, but the producer evidence being proved cannot be replaced with fabricated acknowledgements or completed joins.

The independent A/B descriptor/ID/amount oracle (`:171,183–184,202`) also closes the arrival-order shortcut. The actual GSD off/shadow connector trace is required in addition to its input/options fixture (`:182,208`). Repair coverage includes failed and uncredited calls. Failure/cancellation tests must use the reachable engine semantics rather than an impossible cancelled stepDone acknowledgement (`:175,205`).

Implementation tests and the authorized real-provider shadow build remain future work. The live-fire requirement is explicitly outstanding and cannot be ticked by deterministic controlled-inference tests (`:186,209`). That honest outstanding completion evidence does not block implementation of this blueprint.
