## S1 (unwired, STRAT-AGENT-PEER-1, stratum ca58826 → 7c1f04e): PASS
- **The 3 unwired targets are flagged, and they drop out at the wiring SHA.**
- **The rule:** exported function with no production CALLS+TESTS+REFERENCES and no registry mention. It flags 26 of 232, of which:
  - 6 are the incident
  - 9 are true unwired exports
  - 4 are test hooks
  - **7 are FPs (27%), all from dynamic `await import()`**
- **SM's `dead_code` cannot express this check:**
  - it exempts exports (`dead_code.py:70`)
  - it counts test callers as liveness (`:10,138-145`)

  So forge needs a production-only liveness rule, or SM options for it.

## S2 (missed caller, COMP-ROADMAP-ARCHIVE, compose 90a3ede8): PARTIAL
- **C1:** all 4 `renderRoadmap` sites are `provider.renderRoadmap()` on a local receiver. They show only as unresolved call text, never as graph edges (`ts_bindings.py:99-102`).
- **C48:** 1 of 3 resolve. The other two use dynamic import, which is evidence only (`ts_parser.py:776-784`).
- **C15/C53** are writers/readers of the same artifact, not callers. A "writers of file X" heuristic surfaced them.
- **Verdict: callers-of-X via the graph is not reliable for forge until local-receiver member calls and dynamic imports resolve.** Name-matched call text gives full recall at name-only confidence.

## S3 (nonexistent names, COMP-GSD-2 blueprint, compose d1ee8cde): PASS
