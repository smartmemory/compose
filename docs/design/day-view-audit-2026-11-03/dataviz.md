Captures: `dataviz-1440.png` (dark, the app default), `dataviz-1440-light.png` (light, forced by removing the `dark` class), `dataviz-400.png`. The shared Playwright MCP browser was locked by the parallel auditors, so these were taken with an isolated headless Playwright (same URL, same viewports). Palette numbers come from `scripts/validate_palette.js` in the dataviz skill. Span counts come from the live `/api/ribbon?from=2026-11-02T18:00+08:00&to=2026-11-04T06:00+08:00` (work 27, wait/review 21, wait/gate 10, wait/unknown 5, notRecorded 32, owner 0, marks: land 78, deploy 7).

## What the screen is for (2 lines, your reading)
A swimlane timeline over a 36h window: one row per lane, showing working vs waiting spans, landings and deploys, and what each lane is doing at NOW with an ETA.
Below it a seat and money meter tells the owner how much quota each seat has left and what the last hour cost.

## Three things it does well
- The form is right. A shared time axis with lanes as rows, state as spans, events as glyph marks, a NOW rule and a shaded future is the correct chart for "what moved today", and the future half is already reserved for current, next and ETA.
- The theme work is deliberate. Each mode has its own hex set (DayRibbon.jsx:24-29), not an automatic flip. Texture exists as secondary encoding, and the legend swatches reuse the same pattern defs, so the legend cannot drift from the bars (DayRibbon.jsx:95-114).
- It is honest about data. "not recorded" is a first-class state, the coverage note ("start stamps on 85% of landings") qualifies the marks, and tooltips enhance rather than gate: focus shows the tip, popovers close on Esc and return focus, and every meter value is printed above its bar.

## One-paragraph direction
Keep the swimlane and fix what it shouts. The owner should see three things before anything else: what is waiting on them, what is actively working, and what lands next. Today the loudest ink goes to the quietest state, because review waits are amber stripes across most of the plot, and the one owner-wait is not drawn at all. Rebuild the state palette around that order. Owner-wait gets the single saturated warm hue with a glyph. Working gets a solid confident fill. Review, gate and unknown waits become thin, quiet baselines that carry their age as text at NOW, each with a distinct angle or hue that passes the validator on both surfaces. Move ETAs onto the time axis as diamonds so the empty future becomes a forecast. Take source-level "not recorded" off the plot. Give the meter a two-word key, keep status color for over-pace only, and let it grow at phone width instead of hiding the hottest seat behind a nested scroll.
